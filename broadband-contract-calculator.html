What to compare before signing up

  • Contract length: check how long the advertised price applies and what happens at the end.
  • Setup costs: include activation, delivery, installation or other one-off fees where applicable.
  • Price changes: read the provider's terms for any planned or permitted in-contract increases. This calculator does not forecast unknown increases.
  • Credits and vouchers: check the eligibility rules and when any credit is applied before subtracting it from your cost.
  • Exit terms: understand what happens if you leave before the minimum term.

How the estimate works

The calculator multiplies the monthly fee by the contract length, adds the setup fees you enter and subtracts the bill credits you enter. It then divides the total by the number of months to show an effective monthly average. It does not include optional add-ons, equipment not entered, future price rises or charges you have not included.

For information about broadband availability and connection options, use Ofcom's phones and broadband guidance. Check any specific offer directly with the provider before ordering.
