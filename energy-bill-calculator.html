Estimate your annual energy bill.

Use the electricity and gas usage, unit rates and daily standing charges shown on your own bill. This calculator turns those figures into an illustrative yearly cost — it does not look up live tariffs.

Annual energy cost calculator

Your annual estimate will appear here.

What figures do you need?

Annual usage (kWh): use your latest annual statement or your supplier's estimate. If you only have a monthly figure, multiply it by 12 for a rough estimate, but seasonal energy use varies.

Unit rate: this is the price for each kilowatt-hour used. Electricity and gas have different rates, so enter them separately.

Standing charge: this is a daily fixed charge. Electricity and gas standing charges are separate on a dual-fuel bill, which is why this calculator asks for both.

How the estimate is calculated

For each fuel, the tool multiplies annual kWh by the unit rate (converted from pence to pounds), then adds 365 days of standing charges. The two fuel totals are added together. The result excludes any discounts, credits, taxes or adjustments not represented in the figures you enter.

Before switching supplier

  • Compare the unit rates and standing charges, not only the estimated monthly direct debit.
  • Check whether your usage estimate matches your household's actual consumption.
  • Read the tariff length, exit-fee terms and any other conditions directly with the supplier.
  • Use an up-to-date bill and confirm all figures before making a decision.
