If we later join an affiliate programme, we may receive a commission when a visitor follows a clearly labelled advertising link and completes a qualifying action. A commission would be paid by the partner and should not increase the price paid by the visitor, subject to the partner's terms.

How commercial links will be labelled

Any content or link that is advertising will be clearly identified before a visitor clicks it. A general note on this page will not replace labels beside individual advertising links or offers.

Editorial approach

Calculator results are based on the figures entered by the visitor, not commission rates. Partner approval and commission do not guarantee that a deal is suitable for an individual household.
