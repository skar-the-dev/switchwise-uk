SIM-only or a handset plan?

A SIM-only deal and a phone contract are not always directly comparable. A handset plan may include a substantial upfront payment, a longer minimum term or separate airtime and device agreements. Compare the total amount you expect to pay over the same period.

Check the small print

  • Confirm the minimum term and whether the airtime and handset agreements have different end dates.
  • Check whether the monthly price can change during the contract.
  • Look at data allowances, roaming, speed limits and charges for going over your allowance.
  • Include upfront fees, delivery charges and any optional add-ons you actually need.
  • Check what happens at the end of the minimum term and whether the plan continues at the same price.

How the estimate works

The calculator multiplies the monthly price by the contract length, then adds the handset payment and any one-off fees you enter. It divides the total by the number of months to show an average monthly cost. It does not include unentered add-ons or future price changes.
