ABOUT SWITCHWISE UK

Useful tools, without the noise.

SwitchWise UK is an independent educational toolkit. You enter your own figures and calculations run in your browser. We do not currently provide live tariff comparisons, supplier rankings or personal recommendations. Results are illustrative and may exclude taxes, discounts, usage changes, price rises, fees or other contract terms unless you enter them yourself. Always verify the full terms directly with the provider.

Are the calculators free?

Yes. There is no account or payment required to use the tools.

Are these live prices?

No. The calculators use the figures you type in and do not fetch provider prices.

Do you store my bills?

The calculators run in your browser and do not send entered figures to a SwitchWise server.
